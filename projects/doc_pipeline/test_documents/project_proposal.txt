PROJECT PROPOSAL: AI-Powered Customer Support System
Prepared by: Dr. Emily Rodriguez, CTO
Organization: CloudByte Solutions
Date: June 28, 2026

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
CloudByte Solutions proposes to build an intelligent customer support 
platform leveraging large language models (LLMs) and retrieval-augmented 
generation (RAG) to automate 70% of customer support tickets. The 
projected ROI is 340% within the first year.

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Our current support team of 45 agents handles an average of 2,500 
tickets per day. Average response time is 4.2 hours, and customer 
satisfaction (CSAT) scores have declined from 4.1 to 3.6 over the 
past two quarters. Annual support costs are approximately $3.8 million.

PROPOSED SOLUTION:
1. Deploy a GPT-4 based chatbot for tier-1 support queries
2. Implement RAG pipeline using Pinecone vector database
3. Build escalation workflow for complex cases
4. Integrate with Zendesk, Slack, and Salesforce

TIMELINE:
Phase 1 (July 2026): Architecture design and data collection
Phase 2 (August 2026): MVP development and internal testing
Phase 3 (September 2026): Beta launch with 20% traffic
Phase 4 (October 2026): Full production deployment

BUDGET REQUEST:
- Cloud infrastructure (AWS): $45,000/year
- API costs (OpenAI, Pinecone): $18,000/year
- Development team (3 engineers x 4 months): $240,000
- Total: $303,000

EXPECTED OUTCOMES:
- Reduce ticket volume by 70% within 6 months
- Improve CSAT from 3.6 to 4.5
- Reduce average response time from 4.2 hours to under 30 seconds
- Annual savings of $2.1 million in support costs

RISKS:
- LLM hallucination in regulated industry responses
- Data privacy compliance (GDPR, CCPA)
- Customer resistance to AI-only support

APPROVAL REQUIRED BY: July 15, 2026
